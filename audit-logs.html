Audit integrity principles

Production requirements to preserve trust in the log.
Server-side event capture
Record actions in Laravel after authorization and outcome are known—not from client-side display events alone.
Tamper-resistant storage
Restrict write/delete access, protect backups and consider hash chaining or a dedicated append-only log store.
Actor and context
Capture actor ID, UTC timestamp, action, target record, result, request/correlation ID and approved client context.
Minimize sensitive data
Do not log passwords, session tokens, private keys, full message bodies or sensitive uploaded content.
Filters on this page are for demonstration. Production access to logs should be restricted and itself audited.
