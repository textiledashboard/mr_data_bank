Classification guidelines

Keep the taxonomy useful and easy to search.
01
Use stable codes

Use a short unique code, for example HANDLOOM or TRADE-EXP. Avoid changing codes once the category is in use.

02
Keep the hierarchy shallow

Use a parent category and one sub-category level so researchers can classify files consistently.

03
Deactivate before removing

Categories linked to files cannot be deleted in this demo. Reclassify linked files first, then deactivate or remove unused categories.
